Privacy Policy for Neon Galactic

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

KINGPLUG built the Neon Galactic mobile game as a standalone offline application. This page informs players of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

1. Information Collection and Use

Neon Galactic does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.

  • No account registration or login is required.
  • No analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers are included in the app.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or device identifiers are accessed.

2. Local Game Data

The game stores your local high score and sound mute preference solely on your device using standard local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game.

3. Children's Privacy

Because Neon Galactic does not collect any personal information from any users, it complies with children's privacy standards including COPPA and GDPR-K.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
